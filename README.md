# Plate of Hope

Plate of Hope is a C-based application designed to reduce food wastage by connecting surplus food with people and organizations in need. It manages food donations, quantities, expiry times, and recipient requirements. It uses logical decision-making to distribute food efficiently, provide expiry warnings, and generate distribution reports.
# Objectives of the Project
The main objectives are:
1. To reduce food wastage by encouraging the redistribution of surplus food.
2. To connect food donors with organizations and people in need.
3. To maintain records of available food donations and their quantities.
4. To prioritize food according to its expiry time.
5. To distribute food according to the requirements of recipients.
6. To generate reports showing donated, distributed, and remaining food.
7. To promote social responsibility and help reduce hunger.

# Working of Plate of Hope – Step-by-Step
Step 1: Start the Program
The user runs the C program, and the main menu of Plate of Hope is displayed.

Step 2: Add Food Donation
The donor enters the food name, available quantity, and expiry time. The program stores these details.

Step 3: Add Recipient Details
The user enters the recipient or organization's name, required food quantity, and urgency level (Low, Medium, or High).

Step 4: Display Records
The system displays all registered food donations and recipients along with their details.

Step 5: Check Expiry Warnings
The program checks the expiry time of donated food and displays warnings for food that may expire soon.

Step 6: Redistribute Food
The program compares the available food quantity with the recipient's remaining requirement and distributes the appropriate quantity.

Step 7: Generate Final Report
The system calculates and displays the total food donated, total food distributed, remaining food, and recipient-wise distribution status.

Step 8: Exit the Program
After completing all operations, the user selects the exit option to close the program.
